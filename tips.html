🌾 Farming Tips

Simple practices that can help farmers manage crops effectively.

💧

Water Management

Provide water according to the crop requirement. Avoid over-irrigation and reduce unnecessary water usage.

🌱

Soil Care

Maintain soil fertility by using suitable organic matter and nutrients according to soil conditions.

🔄

Crop Rotation

Rotate crops across seasons to support soil health and reduce repeated pressure from pests and diseases.

🌾

Quality Seeds

Use good-quality seeds that are suitable for the local season, soil and farming conditions.

🐛

Pest Monitoring

Regularly observe crops for pests and diseases and take suitable preventive measures when required.

📅

Proper Timing

Follow suitable sowing, irrigation and harvesting timings according to the selected crop.
