🌱 Crop Guide

Learn about common crops and their basic growing requirements.

🌾 Cotton

Cotton is mainly grown during the Kharif season. It grows well in black soil and requires medium water availability.

  • Kharif Season
  • Black Soil
  • Medium Water
  • 150–180 days

🌱 Soybean

Soybean is a Kharif crop that grows well in black soil with medium water availability.

  • Kharif Season
  • Black Soil
  • Medium Water
  • 90–120 days

🥜 Groundnut

Groundnut is suitable for Kharif conditions and grows well in red soil with medium water.

  • Kharif Season
  • Red Soil
  • Medium Water
  • 100–120 days

🌾 Rice

Rice is a major Kharif crop that grows well in clay soil and requires high water availability.

  • Kharif Season
  • Clay Soil
  • High Water
  • 120–150 days

🌽 Maize

Maize can be grown during the Kharif season and prefers loamy soil with medium water.

  • Kharif Season
  • Loamy Soil
  • Medium Water
  • 90–120 days

🌾 Wheat

Wheat is mainly grown during the Rabi season and grows well in loamy soil with medium water.

  • Rabi Season
  • Loamy Soil
  • Medium Water
  • 120–150 days
